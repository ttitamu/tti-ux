::tux-author-byline{name="Dr. Marcus Vance" role="Senior Research Scientist" affiliation="TTI Network Systems Division"}

::tux-abstract
This paper presents empirical findings from an 18-month longitudinal study monitoring 42 instrumented roadside perception units along Interstate 35. By combining 64-beam roadside LiDAR with multi-access edge computing (MEC), detection latency was reduced below 22ms. We observe a 31% decrease in primary corridor shockwaves during peak commuter periods.
::
