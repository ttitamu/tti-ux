## Priority Focus Areas

::tux-card
### Connected Freight Corridors
Deploying vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication nodes along the I-35 and I-10 Texas corridors to optimize automated freight platoon routing and minimize logistics emissions.

::tux-big-stat{value="34" suffix="%" label="Reduction in stop-wave congestion" tone="maroon" size="sm"}
::
::

::tux-card
### Edge Vision for Vulnerable Road Users
Deploying embedded edge AI at high-incident signalized intersections. Real-time inference tracks pedestrian crosswalk dwell times and triggers adaptive pedestrian clearance intervals.

::tux-big-stat{value="99.2" suffix="%" label="Pedestrian detection accuracy in adverse weather" tone="gold" size="sm"}
::
